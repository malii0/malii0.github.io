About Me

I'm Muhammed Ali, an undergraduate student at Middle East Technical University, Department of Mathematics.

I am interested in computational mathematics, flight dynamics, and optical/astrodynamic modeling. Currently, I contribute to the Galaxy Rocket Team, developing 6-DOF flight dynamics models using MATLAB/Simulink.

Recently, I completed a research internship at the Türkiye National Observatory (TUG) under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Cahit Yeşilyaprak. My work focused on general telescope optics, Adaptive Optics (Zernike Polynomials), and Space Situational Awareness (SSA), encompassing orbital mechanics and orbit determination.
