Reading Log

A list of books, academic papers, and technical literature I've read and referenced for research and coursework.

Introduction to Adaptive Optics — Robert K. Tyson (2000) SPIE Press. Fundamental background on wavefront sensing, deformable mirrors, and atmospheric turbulence mitigation.
Zernike Polynomials and Their Applications — Kuo Niu & Chao Tian (2022) Journal of Optics, Vol. 24, No. 12. Review on mathematical properties of Zernike polynomials and their applications in optical aberration analysis.
Deep Multi-Task and Representation Learning Method for Atmospheric Turbulence Prediction — V. K. P. Sathia et al. Georgia State University. Application of deep learning models for atmospheric turbulence prediction and correction using focal plane speckle images.
TROIA Adaptive Optics System for DAG Telescope — O. Keskin, L. Jolissaint, A. Bouxin, C. Yeşilyaprak (2020) Proc. SPIE 11448, Adaptive Optics Systems VII. Architectural design and operational setup of the TROIA adaptive optics instrument for DAG.
The Design of an Adaptive Optics Telescope: The Case of DAG — L. Jolissaint et al. System-level optical engineering considerations and structural integrated adaptive optics design for the 4m DAG telescope.
Ground-Based Optical Observation System for LEO Objects — T. Yanagisawa, H. Kurosaki, H. Oda, M. Tagawa (2015) Advances in Space Research / JAXA. Observational methodologies, tracking algorithms, and optical architectures for LEO space debris surveillance.
Active Optics System for the 4m Telescope of the Eastern Anatolia Observatory (DAG) — G. P. Lousberg et al. AMOS / ATASAM. Mechanical and optical design of the active optics control system regulating the primary mirror shape at DAG.
