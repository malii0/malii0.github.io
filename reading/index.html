Reading Log

A chronological list of books, academic papers, and technical literature I've read, along with personal notes.

Statistical Orbit Determination — Tapley, Schutz, Born Focusing heavily on batch least-squares estimation and state propagation. Extremely thorough mathematical formulation of orbit determination algorithms.
Principles of Adaptive Optics — Robert Tyson Great introductory text for wavefront sensing methods, deformable mirrors, and atmospheric turbulence modeling. Particularly insightful on Shack-Hartmann principles.
