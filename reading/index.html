Papers, articles, videos — anything worth a short note when I come across it. Newest first.

Introduction to Adaptive Optics book · Robert K. Tyson (2000)

SPIE Press. Fundamental background on wavefront sensing, deformable mirrors, and atmospheric turbulence mitigation.

Zernike Polynomials and Their Applications paper · Kuo Niu & Chao Tian (2022)

Journal of Optics, Vol. 24, No. 12. Review on mathematical properties of Zernike polynomials and their applications in optical aberration analysis.

Deep Multi-Task and Representation Learning Method for Atmospheric Turbulence Prediction paper · V. K. P. Sathia et al.

Georgia State University. Application of deep learning models for atmospheric turbulence prediction and correction using focal plane speckle images.

TROIA Adaptive Optics System for DAG Telescope paper · O. Keskin, L. Jolissaint, A. Bouxin, C. Yeşilyaprak (2020)

Proc. SPIE 11448, Adaptive Optics Systems VII. Architectural design and operational setup of the TROIA adaptive optics instrument for DAG.

The Design of an Adaptive Optics Telescope: The Case of DAG paper · L. Jolissaint et al.

System-level optical engineering considerations and structural integrated adaptive optics design for the 4m DAG telescope.

Ground-Based Optical Observation System for LEO Objects paper · T. Yanagisawa, H. Kurosaki, H. Oda, M. Tagawa (2015)

Advances in Space Research / JAXA. Observational methodologies, tracking algorithms, and optical architectures for LEO space debris surveillance.
